# Product

The lab is a portfolio of interactive web experiments, made to show to other people. Each piece is small, stands alone and is built around one interaction. A piece succeeds when that interaction feels right.

## Users

People who are shown a piece and try it, usually one piece at a time. They arrive with no instructions beyond what the piece itself gives them. The lab is not for people looking for a finished product or tool.

## Vision

A growing set of pieces, each worth showing because of how its interaction feels. Feel is judged by trying a piece: measurements can support that judgement, not replace it.

## Scope

The lab holds standalone interactive pieces that run in a browser.

It deliberately leaves out:

- Production polish, such as accounts, analytics and support for old browsers.
- Maintaining finished pieces. A piece stays as it is unless someone chooses to return to it.

## Principles

- **Feel over features.** Improve how the core interaction feels before adding anything. This rules out options and interface that dilute it.
- **Keep each piece whole.** A piece can be read, changed and shown on its own. This rules out changing one piece as a side effect of working on another.
