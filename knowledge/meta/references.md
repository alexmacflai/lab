# References

Supplied material is what the project receives rather than writes, such as designs, PDFs, screenshots or a client's brief.

- Keep it in a `references/` folder inside the folder it informs: `knowledge/<domain>/references/`, or `knowledge/references/` when it informs the whole project. A `references/` folder is not a domain and needs no approval.
- Keep originals unchanged. Save edited versions, such as crops or annotations, beside them under a new name.
- Its `README.md` lists each file, where it came from and how it informs the work.
- Nothing the project runs or ships depends on a reference. Copy what is needed into the project's own files.
