# Copy editing

Use with [humanise-copy](SKILL.md). Follow the project's [copy rules](../../../knowledge/copywriting/copy-rules.md) and [tone guidance](../../../knowledge/copywriting/voice-and-tone.md).

## Broad-pattern pass

Read for meaning, audience and intended action. Remove inflated claims, vague abstractions, unsupported attribution, unnecessary setup and repetitions that obscure the point. Preserve specifics, uncertainty and strong lines. Flag when a precise replacement needs an unknown fact; do not invent evidence.

## Focused-pattern pass

Inspect binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, fake-insight setups, superficial explanatory phrases, repetitive sentence shapes, artificial rhythm and redundant endings. Edit only patterns weakening this text. Do not mechanically ban words, force short sentences or remove deliberate humour, poetic ambiguity or uneven cadence.

## Comparison

After each pass, identify its changes separately. Check names, numbers, claims, jokes, uncertainty, action semantics and promises against the comparison text. “Archive” must not become “delete”; an error must not promise recovery the prototype cannot provide. For microcopy, clarity matters more than adding personality.

Use relevant approved samples from the prototype rather than treating other prototypes as its voice. These patterns are editing prompts, not evidence of AI authorship.
