# Pen Pals

Five autonomous pens draw on a 320×200 canvas in a DOS-style CGA look, and you can draw alongside them. It opens with something already on the page. The canvas scales by whole numbers to fit the window, so pixels stay hard-edged.

## How to use

- Drag on the canvas to draw.
- Click a pen in the legend, or press `1` to `5`, to turn it on or off.
- Press Space to pause and resume, and `C` to clear the canvas.
- Press `-` and `+` to set the speed from 1 to 9.

With reduced motion requested, it starts at the lowest speed.
