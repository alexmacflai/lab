# Micro-thoughts

A short poem, "Fish air", on a page whose layout changes as you move the pointer or blink.

## How to use

Choose Mouse or Camera on the start screen. Esc returns to it and stops the chosen mode.

- **Mouse:** an invisible grid covers the page. Moving to another cell switches to a different rendering of the poem.
- **Camera:** one face is tracked, and each blink changes to a random layout. A layout can return only after three others. The video stays on your device.

In Camera mode, a floating view follows your face with its mesh drawn on it. You can resize it from its top corners, and a short status line turns green when ready. If the camera cannot be used, a notice offers to try again, use the mouse or go back.
